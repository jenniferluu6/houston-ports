Where the boxes go

All 19 scheduled container services calling Port Houston, drawn along their real sea-lane routes — through the Panama and Suez canals, Gibraltar and Malacca.

How to read this. Each colored line is one liner service, following the actual navigable shipping lane between its published ports of call (marine routing, not straight lines). These are sea-lane paths, not live AIS vessel tracks. Where a line seems to touch land it follows a canal (Panama, Suez) or a river to an inland port (Manaus on the Amazon, New Orleans on the Mississippi). A few ambiguous port names in the PDF were interpreted — see notes at the foot of the page.
